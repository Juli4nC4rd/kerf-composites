Residual stress · composites

Measuring residual stress through the thickness of composites.

A stress map over a whole cross-section of a part, ply by ply, obtained with the contour method. Enough to decide on a cure cycle, a repair or a model calibration from measured values rather than assumed ones.

See the method

What it changes

One measurement, three decisions.

Process

Validate a process

Two cure cycles, two stacking sequences, two tools: the stress map shows what each choice leaves inside the part. You decide on a measurement, not on a distortion noticed afterwards.

Diagnosis

Explain a distortion

A part that warps at demoulding or after machining carries the trace of its thermal history. The stress distribution through the thickness says where and why, and what a process change can alter.

Simulation

Calibrate a digital twin

Thermo-elastic models often underestimate stress peaks in laminates. A map measured on the real cross-section gives the calculation a reference to reach and locates the gap to correct.
